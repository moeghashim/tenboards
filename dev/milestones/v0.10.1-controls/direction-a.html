One control recipe: white fill, an inset hairline that stays at 3:1 contrast, and a 1px shadow underneath. Toolbar buttons become ghost, so only the primary action stands out. Selects show only their label until set; an active filter turns accent-tinted and gets a clear (×) button. Focus is a single primary ring. Menus get a search box, group labels, the check on the left, counts and a deeper shadow.
